# Third-party catalogue rights review

Review date: 28 October 2026
Reviewer: Matthias Muhlert, with structural analysis of the supplied workbook
Status: **Completed for the current catalogue structure**

## Scope

The review examined the 193 external-reference rows in `IMS-Compass-v2.6.1-EN.xlsx`. The earlier language-parity review found matching explicit formula structure across the English and German editions after sheet-name and literal mapping. This review concerns the catalogue's structure and wording, not the legal status of every linked external work and not a jurisdiction-specific legal opinion.

## Findings

- The catalogue contains **193 references**: **123 N27** and **70 N42**.
- Their types are **62 management requirements** and **131 reference controls**.
- The fields identify the standard, edition, section or control ID, and source link.
- Each entry uses a short topic in the project's own words. The topics range from **9 to 47 characters**.
- These topic fields do not reproduce full requirement paragraphs or complete standard tables.
- The `K-...` controls are project-specific building blocks with their own objectives, criteria, check depths, intervals and methods, rather than a copied ISO control catalogue.

## Assessment

The current catalogue consists of factual standard references, identifiers, source links and short original paraphrases. It does not distribute full ISO/IEC requirement text. This structure supports public distribution with the existing source references and attribution. No line-by-line copyright review of all 193 entries is required for this structure.

The author's MIT/CC BY 4.0 allocation applies to original material. Cited standards, research and other third-party works retain their respective rights and terms.

## Decision and future changes

The third-party rights review for the current catalogue structure is complete. Public visibility as a reviewed prerelease is acceptable on this basis.

If future edits add verbatim or substantially similar standard text, complete standard tables, or substantial extracts from linked works, repeat the rights review for that added material.
